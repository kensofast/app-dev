# 🐍 My Favorite Series: *Loki* (Marvel Studios)

> *"It's never too late to change who you are."* – my takeaway from the series

## 📖 Overview
**Loki** is a Marvel Studios series on **Disney+** that premiered in **2021**, created by *Michael Waldron*.
After escaping with the Tesseract, the God of Mischief is captured by the **Time Variance Authority (TVA)**, an organization that protects the "Sacred Timeline."

## 🦹 Main Characters
1. **Loki** 
2. **Mobius M. Mobius** 
3. **Sylvie** 
4. **Ravonna Renslayer**
5. **Ouroboros "O.B."**

## 💭 Why I Love It
- It shows how we can **learn from our own evilness** and grow from it
- Loki goes from ~~villain~~ to **hero**
- He faces his past mistakes
- He learns to care about others
- Great visuals and a very cool TVA retro aesthetic
- The friendship between Loki and Mobius 🤝
